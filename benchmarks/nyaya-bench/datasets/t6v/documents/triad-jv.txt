TRIAD INDUSTRIAL JOINT VENTURE AGREEMENT
Date: November 12, 2025
Parties:
- Meridian Fabrication LLC, a New Jersey limited liability company
- Pell Tooling Inc., a New York corporation
- Shoreline Coatings LP, a Pennsylvania limited partnership

Purpose: Joint bid for the Meadowlands gantry package.

Ownership: Each party holds a one-third (1/3) interest. Profits and losses are shared equally.

Management: Shoreline Coatings LP is the managing venturer for day-to-day operations.

Assignment: No party may assign its interest without unanimous written consent of Meridian, Pell, and Shoreline.

Governing Law: New Jersey.

Notices: To each party's registered office. This Agreement does not attach a bonding exhibit.
