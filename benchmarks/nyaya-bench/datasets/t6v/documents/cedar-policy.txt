CEDAR PAYROLL PARTNERS LLP — WAGE AND HOUR POLICY (INTERNAL)
Effective: January 1, 2024
Applies: New York hourly staff.

Overtime: Non-exempt employees receive overtime after eight (8) hours in a workday, even if weekly hours are under forty.

Meal periods: Thirty minutes unpaid after five hours.

This internal policy is not signed by Marisol Vega. It is a Cedar Payroll handbook excerpt, not a statute.
